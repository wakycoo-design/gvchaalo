THE TREK

Kedarkantha is the classic first snow trek. Pine forests buried in snow, a frozen lake camp at Juda ka Talab, and a pre-dawn summit push for a 360° view over Swargarohini, Bandarpoonch and Black Peak. Short, beautiful and right for first-timers with a basic fitness routine.

ALTITUDE PROFILE

DAY BY DAY

DAY1
Dehradun → Sankri
6,400 ft · 200 km drive · 9–10 hrs

Pick-up at Dehradun at 6:30 AM. A scenic drive along the Yamuna and Tons rivers to the village of Sankri. Briefing and gear check in the evening.

DAY2
Sankri → Juda ka Talab
9,100 ft · 4 km · 5 hrs

Climb through pine and oak forest to camp beside a frozen lake.

DAY3
Juda ka Talab → Kedarkantha Base
11,250 ft · 4 km · 4 hrs

Open snowfields with the summit in sight. Early dinner and an early night.

DAY4
Summit day → Hargaon
12,500 ft · 10 km · 9–10 hrs

Start at 3 AM by headlamp and reach the top for sunrise over the Garhwal range. Then descend to Hargaon camp.

DAY5
Hargaon → Sankri → Dehradun
6 km trek · 200 km drive

A short walk down, then the drive back. Expect to reach Dehradun around 8–9 PM.
